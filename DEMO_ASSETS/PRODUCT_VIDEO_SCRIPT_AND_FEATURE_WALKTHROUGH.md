## 📌 Executive Product Positioning & Investment Thesis

**MedicalAI** is an institutional-grade clinical diagnostic co-pilot that resolves the two largest pain points in modern healthcare delivery: **diagnostic diagnostic fatigue leading to human malpractice/oversights**, and **administrative throughput bottlenecks**.

Powered by **Google MedGemma** and multimodal vision LLMs, MedicalAI fuses:
1. **Multi-Slice Volumetric Imaging** (DICOM CT/MRI with Hounsfield Unit attenuation windowing)
2. **Sub-Visual Pathology & Epiluminescence** (Polarized contact dermoscopy, H&E biopsy whole-slide microscopy, 50° retinal funduscopy)
3. **Unstructured Clinical Text Extraction** (Automated multi-page PDF ingestion & OCR segmentation)
4. **The Human Error & Discrepancy Engine** (Algorithmic cross-verification catching laterality mismatches, metric contradictions, and dropped critical findings)
5. **NCBI PubChem Pharmacological Grounding** (Direct drug intelligence, active compound verification, and institutionally branded prescription synthesis)
