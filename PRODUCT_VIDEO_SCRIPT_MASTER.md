## 📌 Executive Summary & Production Directives

This document provides the definitive, frame-by-frame production blueprint for creating a world-class institutional demo video of the **MedicalAI Platform**. 

### Critical Execution Rules:
1. **Zero UI Cutoffs**: Every interactive panel, table, and modal is staged within the active 16:9 frame with comfortable padding. No features are clipped at the edges.
2. **Dynamic Cursor Zoom-In**: When performing a click or key action, the camera executes a smooth 1.4× to 1.7× zoom-in centered on the cursor position with cubic bezier easing (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`), lingering for 0.4s to highlight the interaction, then gracefully re-centering to full frame.
3. **Sound Design (SFX)**: Every tactile event (mouse click, keyboard typing, file drop, page transition, AI inference laser sweep, and prescription print mechanical hum) has a synchronized, high-fidelity audio cue.
4. **Real Clinical Data Across All Screens**: Strict adherence to authentic medical parameters across all three departments (Radiology, Dermatology, Ophthalmology). Patient names, MRNs, vitals, ICD-10 diagnostic codes, Hounsfield windowing, and NIH PubChem pharmacological dosages match with zero discrepancies.
5. **No Placeholders or Mock Data**: All diagnostic imagery and PDF documents correspond directly to the platform's production assets in `/sample_scans` and `/DEMO_ASSETS`.
